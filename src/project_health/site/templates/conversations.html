What's coming

Phase 2a — interaction health

Classified metrics over dev@, user@, JIRA comments and GitHub PR comments, gated on a human-labeled benchmark corpus and the classifier clearing agreement/precision thresholds against it (D1). See docs/spec/COMMUNITY-HEALTH.md for the full spec.

These measure interaction health, not raw sentiment — a heated technical disagreement is not, by itself, unhealthy. The classifier looks at how disagreements are conducted (do threads reach resolution, do people engage in good faith), not at emotional tone.

Phase 2b — ASF Slack

Aggregate-only metrics from ASF Slack, gated on PMC consensus on dev@ and ASF Infra approval of a read-only bot on public channels (D1). Raw Slack text is never committed to this repo or published; only aggregate counts persist, with no quotes, no links and no individual attribution.
